# Suds & Snouts Dog Grooming

## Product
A one-to-one, appointment-only dog grooming salon on the high street in Naas, Co. Kildare, Ireland. One dog in the salon at a time, no cages. Website: single public marketing page with an online booking request flow, plus privacy and 404 pages.

## Audience
Dog owners in Naas, Sallins, Kill, Johnstown, Clane, Newbridge and wider Co. Kildare. Many own fluffy "poo" crosses (cockapoos, cavapoos, doodles) needing grooms every 4 to 8 weeks; many have nervous, elderly or reactive dogs; new puppy owners. Mostly browsing on phones.

## Job of the site
Get a local owner to book a groom (online request or phone call: 087 500 4500, hello@sudsandsnouts.com), and rank for "dog grooming Naas" and related local searches.
