Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Suds & Snouts Dog Grooming ("we", "us") is a dog grooming salon in Naas, Co. Kildare. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and your rights under the GDPR and the Irish Data Protection Acts.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile number and email address.
  • Your dog's name, breed, age, health and behaviour notes, vet and vaccination details.
  • Your appointment history and any payments or deposits.
  • Photos of your dog, only shared online with your permission.

Why we use it

  • To book, confirm and remind you about appointments (by text, call or email).
  • To groom your dog safely, based on the health and behaviour notes you give us.
  • To keep accounts and meet our legal obligations.

We use your information to provide the service you asked for (contract), to meet legal obligations, and with your consent for things like sharing photos. We never sell your information or use it for third-party marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep client records while you're an active client and for up to 2 years after your last visit, unless the law requires us to keep accounting records for longer.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that help us run the salon, such as our booking, text messaging and email providers, under contracts that protect your information. We may share details with your vet in an emergency involving your dog.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information, object to how we use it, or withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@sudsandsnouts.com or call 087 500 4500. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data you can contact the Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.

Cookies

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
